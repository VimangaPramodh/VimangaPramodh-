### About me

I started in 2018 as a trader who got bored doing the same clicks over and over — so I automated my own workflow. Eight years later that habit turned into a full multi-platform trading business. I design and build automated trading systems on top of the Deriv API, run a network of live platforms, and support sub-partners under my Master Partner account.

- 🤖 I build **automated trading bots** and **fintech web apps**
